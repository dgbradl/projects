How to play

Goal: reach $10,000.

1. Place a Parts Depot — it imports raw parts (for a fee) onto the conveyor it faces.
2. Run conveyors into an Assembler (2 parts → 1 widget). Face its output at more conveyors.
3. End the line at a Shipping Dock to sell at market price.
4. Hire workers to staff machines. Later, feed widgets + chips into a Fabricator for high-margin gadgets.
